Cloud Crate

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Cloud Crate is a media player for Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built to keep your information on your own devices. We do not run servers that collect your data, we do not have user accounts, and we do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your devices

Your music, saved channels and videos, settings and listening history are stored on your devices. If you use iCloud, Cloud Crate syncs your library between your own devices through your iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud services. That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to us.

Information sent to other services

To do its job, the app contacts a few third-party services directly from your device. Only what each request needs is sent:

  • YouTube and YouTube Music: channel, video and album requests you make in the app.
  • Artwork and artist information: an artist's or album's name, sent to Apple Music, TheAudioDB, Deezer, MusicBrainz, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons to find pictures and details.
  • Cloud folders you add: if you add a folder from Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or Box, the app reads it through that provider's own app on your device. We never receive your sign-in details.

Each of those services handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Discord

If you turn on Show What I'm Listening To (off by default), the Mac app sends the song title, artist, album and album art of what you are playing to the Discord app on the same Mac, which shows it on your Discord profile. Nothing is sent to Discord while the setting is off. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Your devices on your network

Cloud Crate on your Mac and iPhone can find each other on the same local network to show and control what each is playing. That connection stays on your local network and is encrypted with a key kept in your own iCloud account, so only your devices can join.

This website

cloudcrate.app is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts, both of which may log standard request information such as your IP address. The site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

Cloud Crate does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent can set a device to a restricted mode that plays only approved content.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact details on cloudcrate.app.
